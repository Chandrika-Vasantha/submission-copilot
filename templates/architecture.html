How a submission flows

Steps in processing order. Each box notes what the prototype does and, where it differs, the production equivalent.

Where AI is used, and where it is not

  • Classification: deterministic first; the LLM only sees pages with a weak margin.
  • Extraction: deterministic parsers + validators; the vision model re-reads scans whose OCR is weak or whose numbers fail a check (e.g. IFTA rows that don't sum to the printed total).
  • Analysis: the LLM explains, spots patterns and drafts the agent email, with evidence citations.
  • Decisions: never the LLM. The YAML rules decide; people confirm.

Confidence

  • Base by method: spreadsheet 97%, text layer 95%, OCR 85% scaled by tesseract word confidence, vision 88%.
  • Validators raise or cut it: totals reconcile, VIN check digit, dates parse.
  • Agreement across documents raises it; a conflict drops it to 60% of the best source until a person resolves it.
  • Findings: capped by evidence; uncited LLM findings capped at 40%.
